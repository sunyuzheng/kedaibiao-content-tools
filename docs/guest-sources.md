# Guest interview sources

Public guest identities, programme relationships and published reading links are maintained in [Open Context](https://github.com/sunyuzheng/lizheng-open-context). Its `catalog/guests.json` supplies both the website and retrieval service.

This project owns local media, original subtitles, metadata acquisition and review evidence. `guests.json` and `guest_video_metadata.json` are inputs for channel maintenance tools. New public facts are reconciled into Open Context's catalog.

## Source records

Each programme has its actual source ID, URL, title and publication date. A YouTube source has a video ID; a Circle source uses its published URL. Recording date and publication date are separate facts. Community resources and companion articles link to their published owners.

## Update

1. Verify the local source and public metadata.
2. Update the public catalog in Open Context and validate its release.
3. Review the exact public change and destination.
4. Consumers synchronize the approved commit and manifest, then build and verify their outputs.

See [the shared architecture](https://github.com/sunyuzheng/lizheng-open-context/blob/main/docs/conversation-architecture.md), [website maintenance](网站嘉宾维护手册.md), and [the editorial workflow](guest-insights-workflow.md).
